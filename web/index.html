Share medical records without sharing the patient

Names, phone numbers, addresses, dates and ID numbers are replaced with labels. Symptoms, diagnoses, medications and lab values stay as they were.

  • Removed: personal identifiers (HIPAA Safe Harbor)
  • Kept: the medical content

Upload records

One file opens as one record. Several files, or a CSV file with one patient per row, become a research table.

Records are processed in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
